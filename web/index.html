🎯 What This Project Does

This is an automated Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) data quality pipeline. It takes your raw monthly or master CSV files, automatically inspects them for errors, standardizes strings and dates, drops duplicates, fixes missing non-critical attributes, isolates unrecoverable bad data into a quarantine folder, loads clean records into a relational database, and computes executive analytics reports and trend charts.

📝 Step-by-Step Instructions: How to Use

  1. Upload your CSV files: Go to the "🚀 Pipeline & Upload" tab, select your CSV files (e.g. Customers.csv, Products.csv, Sales.csv), and click "Upload Files to data/raw/".
  2. Run the Pipeline: Click the blue "Run Pipeline" button in the top header or in the upload tab.
  3. Review Data Quality: Check the "🛡 Data Quality & Quarantine" tab to see what was cleaned vs what bad rows were quarantined.
  4. View Visual Reports: Switch to the "📊 Analytics Dashboard" tab to view charts, revenue trends, and regional breakdown.
  5. Execute SQL Analytics: Go to "⚡ SQL Studio" to run advanced queries with CTEs and Window Functions (e.g., product ranking with RANK(), month-over-month growth with LAG(), and running spend with SUM() OVER).

📋 Supported CSV File Formats

You can upload separate dimension and fact files, or a single combined sales file:

File Type Example Filename Required Columns What The Pipeline Does
Customers Customers.csv customer_id, customer_name, region Cleans names, standardizes regions, and upserts into master customers table.
Products Products.csv product_id, product_name, category Standardizes product catalog and categories, upserts into products table.
Sales (Fact) Sales.csv or monthly CSVs customer_id, product_id, sale_date, quantity, sales_amount Validates FKs, cleans numbers & dates, checks bounds, and inserts into sales table.
Combined Sales monthly_sales.csv All columns above combined Automatically separates dimension records from sales facts and populates all tables.

🛡 The 6 Data Quality Rules Applied

  • 1. Missing Values: Rejects records missing critical IDs/dates; auto-imputes non-critical blanks (e.g. unknown region).
  • 2. Duplicate Detection: Identifies and removes duplicate transactions while logging count.
  • 3. Range Validation: Rejects negative amounts (sales_amount ≤ 0) and zero/negative quantities (quantity ≤ 0).
  • 4. Date Validation: Converts valid dates to standard format; flags corrupt dates or future-dated records.
  • 5. Foreign Key Integrity: Verifies customer and product references exist before inserting sales facts.
  • 6. Type Coercion: Cleans currency formatting (e.g., "$1,200.50" ➔ 1200.50).
